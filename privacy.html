Glowscope

Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Glowscope works on photographs of your face, so this policy is specific about where those photos go. The short version: they are analysed and discarded, they are never stored on our servers, and the copies you keep stay on your phone.

What we collect

  • Photos you take in the app. Face scans, and photos of product labels.
  • Your answers to the three setup questions: your main concern, your skin type, and how reactive your skin is.
  • Purchase status — whether you have an active subscription or trial.

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Glowscope has no account system, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.

Where your photos go

When you take a scan, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis function, which passes it to Anthropic, our AI processor, to produce your results. The same happens for product label photos.

Your photos are not stored on our servers. They are held only for as long as the analysis takes, and are discarded once the results come back.

Your photos are not used to train AI models and are not used for advertising or marketing.

The copy of the photo you see in the app — the one attached to your scan history and used for before-and-after comparisons — is written to your device's private app storage and never uploaded.

What stays on your device

Your scan history, your scores, your findings, your routine and your progress photos are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private container. We cannot see them. Nobody else can read them.

This has a consequence worth knowing: if you delete Glowscope or change phones, that history is gone and cannot be recovered, because we never had a copy.

Who else is involved

  • Anthropic — analyses scan and product photos. Data sent through their API is not used to train their models.
  • Supabase — hosts the function that passes your photo to the analyser. Photos pass through it and are not retained.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions and receives an anonymous identifier plus your purchase status.
  • Apple — processes the payment. We never see your payment details.

Children

Glowscope is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Deleting the app deletes everything Glowscope holds about you, because everything it holds is on your device. If you want confirmation that nothing of yours remains on our side, email us and we will explain exactly what we do and do not have — which, for scan content, is nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your photos are handled, we will update this page and change the date at the top before the change takes effect.
