Common questions

My scan failed

Analysis needs a network connection. If it fails, tap Try again — most failures are transient. If it keeps failing, take a new photo in better light with your hair back and no glasses.

I changed phones and my history is gone

Scan history and progress photos live only on the device. There is currently no account and no cloud backup, so they do not transfer. We are working on this.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancelling happens in iOS Settings, not in Glowscope: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Glowscope → Cancel Subscription. Cancelling during the free trial means you are never charged.

I already paid but the app says I haven't

Tap Restore on the paywall. If that does not work, email us with the Apple receipt and we will sort it out.

Delete my data

Deleting the app removes every scan and photo, because they are only ever stored on your device. There is no account to delete.
