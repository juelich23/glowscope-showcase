Common questions

Analysis limits

Face analysis is limited to 2 requests per day and 8 in a rolling 30-day period per installation. Failed requests after processing begins may count. Temporary shared capacity limits may apply. Product-label reading and routine planning run on your phone and do not use that allowance.

My scan failed

Analysis needs a network connection. If it fails, tap Try again — most failures are transient. If it keeps failing, take a new photo in better light with your hair back and no glasses.

I changed phones and my history is gone

Use Settings → Backup & transfer to export a password-encrypted Glowscope backup, then import it on your new iPhone. Keep your password safe; we cannot recover it. There is no automatic cloud sync. Your data may also transfer through an iPhone backup or device transfer, depending on your Apple settings. Restore purchases separately and re-enable reminders on the new device.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so cancelling happens in iOS Settings, not in Glowscope: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Glowscope → Cancel Subscription. Follow Apple’s displayed cancellation deadline; cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid renewal.

I already paid but the app says I haven't

Open Today → More → Settings & profile → Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the paywall. If that does not work, email us with the Apple receipt and we will sort it out.

Delete my data

Delete individual scans in Progress, or use Settings → Delete profile, scans & history. Existing device backups and provider-held subscription/analytics records are separate. See our privacy policy for details.
