What it does

  • Scores seven metrics from one photo: breakouts, redness, texture, pores, pigmentation, hydration and the eye area.
  • Builds a morning and evening schedule from your goals, skin type, sensitivity and preferences.
  • Checks verified product labels against your routine’s concentration requirements and ingredient exclusions.
  • Keeps your photos and check-ins together; scores are estimates that can vary with lighting.
