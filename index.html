Glowscope

Skin analysis that tells you the truth

Scan your face, get an honest read on seven skin metrics, and a routine built from what was actually found — not a report card telling you everything is fine.

What it does

  • Scores seven metrics from one photo: breakouts, redness, texture, pores, pigmentation, hydration and the eye area.
  • Builds a morning and evening routine from your own results and how reactive your skin is.
  • Checks products against your results — whether that bottle suits your skin, not whether it is good in general.
  • Tracks your score over time so you can see whether a routine is working.

Glowscope is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for a dermatologist. If something on your skin is changing, painful, or worrying you, see a doctor.
